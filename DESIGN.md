## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an executive, authoritative, and precision-engineered digital presence for high-impact technology leadership. It balances the strategic rigor of an enterprise Delivery Director with the technical pedigree of a Dynamics 365/Power Platform Solution Architect and AI Systems Engineer.

The aesthetic philosophy marries **Executive Modernism** with **Architectural Precision**:
- **Clarity & Authority:** Uncompromising typographic scale and stark legibility project seasoned enterprise stewardship and board-level communication.
- **Engineered Precision:** Strict structural grid alignments, technical hairline dividers, and micro-metric badges communicate deep algorithmic rigor, full-stack systems acumen, and architectural mastery.
- **Restraint & Substance:** Content breathes over an expansive white-to-slate canvas, avoiding superfluous skeuomorphism or loud visual distractions. Surfaces utilize frosted glassmorphism alongside soft, multi-layered ambient shadows to create architectural elevation without visual clutter.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered specifically for a luminous, high-contrast, executive light theme:

- **Canvas & Surface System:**
  - Base Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) establishes subtle warmth and reduces eye fatigue.
  - Surface Pure: `#FFFFFF` (White) defines elevated cards, active panels, and floating elements.
  - Surface Glass: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `16px` backdrop-blur for sticky executive navigation bars and analytical overlays.
  - Surface Subdued: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) for inset wells, code surfaces, and metrics backings.

- **Typography & Structural Neutrals:**
  - Primary Text: `#0F172A` (Deep Slate 900) provides decisive, editorial contrast.
  - Secondary Text: `#334155` (Slate 700) delivers clear narrative body copy.
  - Muted / Supporting Text: `#64748B` (Slate 500) for metadata, architectural tags, and sub-labels.
  - Hairline Borders: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) serves as the crisp baseline structural boundary, with `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) applied on interactive hover.

- **Accents & Domain Identifiers:**
  - **Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`):** The primary authority color. Represents enterprise delivery, architectural scale, links, and primary CTA states.
  - **Emerald Green (`#059669`):** The operational intelligence accent. Applied to AI engineering badges, active delivery statuses, platform telemetry, and success metrics.
  - **Azure Horizon (`#0284C7`):** Tertiary accent for cloud infrastructure, data pipelines, and technical taxonomies.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy communicates editorial executive authority reinforced by engineering precision:

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Chosen for its structural geometries and subtle technical character. High contrast letterspacing (`-0.03em` on display sizes) conveys executive confidence and forward-leaning innovation.
- **Narrative Body (`Inter`):** Selected for its neutral, highly legible rhythm across case studies, architecture briefs, and executive summaries.
- **System Telemetry & Architecture Badges (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied to AI parameters, platform framework tags (e.g., Dataverse, Azure Functions, LLM pipelines), and chronological dates to inject technical authenticity.
- **Pacing Rule:** Body text blocks must maintain a maximum reading line length of 68 characters to preserve reading cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on a deterministic **8px modular grid** embedded inside a fixed-width container system:

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column grid with `max-width: 1280px`, `gutter-desktop: 2rem` (32px), and outer canvas `margin-desktop: 3rem` (48px).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and margins.

- **Vertical Rhythm & Separation:**
  - Section blocks utilize generous vertical intervals (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to create clear cognitive segmentation between major milestones, case studies, and architecture blueprints.
  - Internal card padding follows strict proportional distribution: `space-lg` (24px) for standard cards and `space-xl` (40px) for spotlight executive case studies.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean planar geometry, translucent blurs, and soft, natural multi-tiered shadows rather than high-contrast surfaces:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Completely flat; provides the grounding plane.
- **Level 1 (Structural Card):** `#FFFFFF` with hairline border `1px solid #E2E8F0` and shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Card / Hover State):** `#FFFFFF` with refined border `1px solid #CBD5E1` and compound ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Command Bars):** `#FFFFFF` bordered with `#E2E8F0` and expansive elevation:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 0 1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`.
- **Glassmorphic Shell (Headers & Floating Dock Nav):**
  `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%); border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a **Soft (Level 1)** corner geometry, reflecting an executive architectural balance between clean corporate discipline and modern softness:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form inputs, micro-tags, code callouts, and table cells.
- **Medium Radius (`0.375rem` / 6px):** Buttons, segmented controls, dropdown menus, and standard chips.
- **Card Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Structural cards, project viewports, and architecture layout panels.
- **Large Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Hero feature containers, dialogue modals, and executive spotlight callouts.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Reserved strictly for operational status pips (e.g., active availability badges) and categorical metadata pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Executive Button:** Solid cobalt blue background (`#2563EB`), text `#FFFFFF` in `font-weight: 500`, border-radius `6px`, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Hover state: `#1D4ED8` with `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`. Active: `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary Ghost/Outline Button:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Icon Utility Button:** 36x36px square, radius `6px`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, color `#334155`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Technical Chips (Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Full-Stack):** Background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#334155`, font `JetBrains Mono` at `12px`, radius `4px`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`.
- **AI & Systems Status Badge:** Background `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.25)`, text `#059669`. Prepended with an emerald pulsing status indicator (6px circle).

### Executive Cards
- **Architecture Showcase Card:** Base white surface `#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `1.5rem`, radius `8px`. Top row houses the client/enterprise sector and completion status. Middle section highlights the technical topology with inline tags. Footer presents key quantified delivery KPIs (e.g., "$12M Budget", "99.98% SLA", "45% Efficiency Gain") rendered in `metric-stat` scale.
- **Interactive Shift:** On hover, transition `transform: translateY(-2px)` and shadow Level 2 elevation with a 150ms ease-out duration.

### Lists & Timeline Components
- **Delivery Timeline:** Vertical rule `1px solid #E2E8F0` positioned along the left rail. Nodes feature an 8px circular node with an active state in `#2563EB` bordered by `#FFFFFF` and wrapped in an outer `4px` ring of `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Role Items:** Role title rendered in `Space Grotesk` (`18px`), accompanied by dates in `JetBrains Mono` (`12px`, `#64748B`), and bullet items styled with subtle directional markers (`→` in `#2563EB`).

### Form Controls
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, radius `6px`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`, text `#0F172A`. Focus state: border `#2563EB`, outer ring `3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` with zero outline offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px` dimension, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio). Checked state: `#2563EB` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` check glyph.

### Architecture Blueprint Callouts
- **System Diagram Well:** Inset background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px dashed #CBD5E1`, interior padding `2rem`, radius `8px`. Displays dataflow schematics, enterprise API connections, and Power Apps lifecycle stages with monospaced annotations.
